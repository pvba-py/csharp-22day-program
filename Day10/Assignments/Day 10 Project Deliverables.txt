Phase 4: Database Design 
19 tables. Optimizations applied: ReleaseRequests merged into ReleasedSlots (release event + slot inventory in one row), EmergencyBuffers merged into SystemSettings (per-hospital keys). New: org.Hospitals, scheduling.SchedulingCycles, cycle rollover with CyclesWaited score boost and starvation flag.
Final score formula:
Score = PriorityWeight×50 + min(WaitingDays,30)×2 + ReadinessWeight×20
      + DurationFit×15 + CyclesWaited×10
Starvation flag: CyclesWaited >= 3 → highlighted on scheduler's weekly screen
